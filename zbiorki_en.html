Donations and Foundation Support

Thank you for standing on watch with us!

The mission of the Ostatnia Warta Foundation is to be present with another human being in the most difficult moments of life – in loneliness, terminal illness, and the passing process. We can only pursue this noble challenge thanks to the support of people with big hearts. Your every gesture matters. We invite you – stand with us on this most important watch. Choose a convenient payment method or a targeted fundraiser.
