2. Purposes and Legal Grounds for Data Processing

Your personal data is processed based on the provisions of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for the following purposes:

  • Fulfilling the Foundation's statutory objectives, including providing care for beneficiaries and organizing volunteering (Art. 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).
  • Handling donations and accounting/tax matters, including the accounting of payments made to our bank account and communicating with donors (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
  • Communication and providing responses to inquiries sent electronically, via the contact form, or traditional mail (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest of the Controller).

3. Data Recipients and External Partners (Payments and Fundraisers)

Personal data may be shared with entities supporting the Foundation (e.g., accounting office, hosting and e-mail providers) exclusively on the basis of appropriate data processing agreements.

Important disclaimer regarding payments: The Foundation uses the services of external payment operators and fundraising platforms (including Zrzutka.pl, Patronite, PayPal, FaniMani, BuyCoffee). The moment a User clicks a link redirecting to a chosen support method, they leave the ostatniawarta.pl website. From that point on, the transaction and data collection are subject exclusively to the privacy policy, cookie policy, and terms of service of the given payment operator.

The "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation obtains Donors' personal data from these partners only to the limited extent that they are shared following a successful transaction (usually limited to: first name, last name, e-mail address, and the amount, as described in the Donation Terms). The Foundation does not have access to banking login details or payment card information.

4. Rights of Data Subjects

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access your data and receive a copy of it.
  • The right to rectify (correct) your data.
  • The right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten"), provided there are no other legal contraindications (e.g., the obligation to archive accounting records).
  • The right to restrict processing, the right to data portability, and the right to object.

5. Cookies, Analytics, and External Tracking

Our website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: Necessary for the proper functioning of the website and remembering your cookie consent.
  • Analytical (Google Analytics 4): We use statistical tools to collect anonymized data about website traffic (e.g., number of visits). Google Analytics does not collect data that allows for direct identification (e.g., surnames or exact IP addresses).
  • Tracking and Third-Party Cookies: Please note that external operators we link to (e.g., Zrzutka.pl or payment operators) may use their own cookies and tracking mechanisms (e.g., to measure conversions, analyze payments, or track referral link traffic). When you navigate to a partner's website or interact with their widget, that partner's tracking mechanisms apply. The Foundation may have access to aggregated technical statistics shared by these portals.

You can manage your cookie settings or block them entirely directly in your web browser settings at any time.

This Privacy Policy does not exist in a vacuum and constitutes an integral part of the entire legal framework of our website. Using our website is also subject to the general Terms of Use and – in the case of financial support – the Donation Terms.
