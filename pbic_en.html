At the "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation, we believe that the true power of business lies not only in its financial results but in its real impact on the environment. Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) is not just a buzzword for us – it is a wise partnership that benefits both parties.

We invite your company to support our mission. Your commitment will allow us to reach where support is needed most – bringing relief to the chronically and terminally ill, supporting people with disabilities, and giving lonely seniors the strength to live.

Why become a partner of the Foundation?

Brand image

You will build the position of an empathetic, responsible, and socially engaged company in the eyes of customers, contractors, and employees.

Team integration

Employee volunteering and shared charitable goals are some of the best tools for building a loyal and close-knit team.

Tax benefits

According to Polish law, donations made for the statutory purposes of the Foundation can be deducted from the tax base (CIT/PIT).

Certificate and acknowledgments

We officially honor our partners by providing materials (certificates, logos) that you can use in your own PR communication.

Forms of cooperation – choose a model for your company

No two companies are the same, which is why we are open to flexible forms of partnership:

Strategic and purposeful sponsorship

Regular financial support or funding a specific project/purchase of assistive equipment for our patients.

In-kind and service donations

Donating products, medical equipment, hygiene products, or providing services (e.g., legal, IT, transport) pro publico bono.

Employee volunteering

Involving your team in our ongoing relief activities, logistical support, or help in organizing social events.

Cause Related Marketing

Donating a specific percentage from the sale of your products or services to the Foundation.

Corporate donations and tax deductions

Supporting the "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation is not only a beautiful gesture but also concrete financial benefits. Under tax law, donations made for the statutory purposes of the foundation are deductible from the tax base – up to 10% of income for companies (CIT) and up to 6% for individuals conducting business activity (PIT). Use this mechanism to wisely and effectively implement your corporate social responsibility strategy.
