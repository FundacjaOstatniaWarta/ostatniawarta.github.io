In many moments of life, a person does not need grand words or complicated actions.

What is needed most is the presence of another human being.

The Gdańsk School of Accompanying is an educational initiative of the "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation, born from the need to pass on knowledge, experience, and a mature way of being with another person.

It is a space for formation, reflection, and practical preparation for those who want to help responsibly, wisely, and with full respect for human dignity — both through conversation and presence, as well as through shared action.

Our mission recognized! "Gdańsk School of Accompanying" under the patronage of the International Year of Volunteers 2026

Gdańsk School of Accompanying – an official event of the International Year of Volunteers for Sustainable Development (IVY 2026) celebrations

We are honored to announce that our original training cycle has received the patronage of the Director of the National Institute of Freedom – Centre for the Development of Civil Society (NIW-CRSO) as part of the IVY 2026 celebrations. As part of this undertaking, during 40 hours of classes with educators and psychologists, we thoroughly prepare a group of volunteers to accompany people at the end of life (the so-called "end-of-life doula"). This distinction confirms that the project directly contributes to the implementation of the Sustainable Development Goals, promoting solidarity and professional social support.

Why the Gdańsk School of Accompanying was created

In a world that increasingly avoids difficult topics, we want to create a space that restores the significance of:

  • empathy
  • attentive presence
  • conversations about ultimate matters
  • accompanying in illness and the process of passing
  • shared action as a form of support and relationship

The Gdańsk School of Accompanying was conceived as a place where a person can not only gain knowledge but also mature into being with another person in a responsible and calm manner.

We teach that helping does not always mean doing. Sometimes the greatest support is a calm, conscious presence with another human being.
But we also teach that presence can be expressed through simple, shared action, which restores meaning, rhythm, and a sense of agency.

Educational program

As part of the Gdańsk School of Accompanying, we want to develop a program that includes, among others:

  • basics of communication with a seriously ill person
  • psychological aspects of illness, loneliness, and dying
  • support for family and caregivers
  • ethics of accompanying at the end of life
  • organizing vigils and presence with the sick
  • introduction to the role of an End-of-life Doula
  • building relationships with elderly, sick, or lonely people
  • introduction to occupational therapy workshops as a form of presence and activation

An important element of the program is also occupational therapy workshops, understood not only as a form of activation but as a space for meeting, building relationships, and restoring a person's sense of agency.
We teach that action can also be a form of presence.

Accompanying through action

Not every presence is expressed in conversation.

Sometimes a person needs shared action more than words. Something that restores the rhythm of the day, gives a sense of meaning, and allows them to forget about what is difficult for a moment.

That is why, within the Gdańsk School of Accompanying, we also develop an approach based on occupational therapy workshops.

We understand them as a space for:

  • building relationships through shared action
  • strengthening the sense of agency and self-worth
  • restoring the simple joys of everyday life
  • presence that doesn't require many words

These can be simple, calm, and very human actions — ones that help regain rhythm, agency, and the feeling of still being part of the world.

Because sometimes the most important thing is not what we say.

The most important thing is that we are together — in action, in silence, in ordinary being.

We teach that presence can also be expressed through action.
Because sometimes being together starts with the simplest things.

Courses and training

The "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation is preparing a program of training, courses, and workshops that will allow you to gain knowledge and practical skills in accompanying the sick, the lonely, the elderly, and those at the end of life.

We want participants not only to gain knowledge but also to cultivate maturity, gentleness, and readiness for responsible presence with another human being.

Course participants will be able to obtain:

Certificate of completion of the "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation training

Confirming the acquisition of competencies in non-medical support for the sick, the dying, and their families.

In the future, the program of the Gdańsk School of Accompanying will also be developed with more practical forms — including workshops, formation meetings, and activities based on occupational therapy and activation through presence.

Important: The total income from the sale of our courses and training exclusively funds the statutory goals of the "Ostatnia Warta" Foundation. By choosing education with us, you are tangibly helping our Patients!

Who is this path for

We direct the program of the Gdańsk School of Accompanying to people who:

  • want to become foundation volunteers
  • work in the field of social assistance, care, or education
  • accompany their loved ones in illness, old age, or loneliness
  • wish to gain knowledge about the process of dying, grief, and human fragility
  • want to learn presence also through action and supportive workshops
  • feel a calling to serve as an End-of-life Doula

It is also a space for all those who feel that in today's world, there is a need for more people capable of calm, mature, and faithful presence.
