The mission of the Ostatnia Warta Foundation is to be present for another human being in the most difficult moments of life. We support not only chronically and terminally ill individuals, but also lonely seniors and people struggling with disabilities. We can only accomplish this noble challenge with the support of big-hearted people.

Our task is not medical treatment.
Our task is to be present.

Sometimes this means conversation and spending time together. Sometimes it means helping with everyday matters or social rehabilitation. And sometimes, it's just a quiet vigil by the patient's bed.

Who are we looking for?

We invite people who:

  • want to help in a responsible, balanced, and calm manner,
  • are capable of showing empathy and deep respect for another human being,
  • are ready to dedicate a fraction of their free time to regular volunteering.

We do not require a medical background. Every volunteer undergoes appropriate preparation for their new role through the Foundation's free educational programs.

How to join the Watch?

To streamline the process, we have done away with paper forms in the first stage of recruitment. Simply fill out the short online questionnaire below. We will contact you to arrange an informal chat. Any formalities and legal documents (Agreement, Confidentiality Rules) will only be signed after a mutual decision to start cooperation.
