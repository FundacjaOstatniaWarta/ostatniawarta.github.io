Projects

Thanks to our involvement in various initiatives and grant programs, we are constantly developing our competencies to support our Patients even more effectively.

Representation of the "OSTATNIA WARTA" Foundation at nationwide long-term and senior care events in 2026.

In 2026, our Foundation is implementing a project aimed at exchanging knowledge and building relationships with key entities in the medical and care sector. As part of the task, Board members and volunteers will participate in national congresses to share experience and learn best practices.

Presence at these congresses allows us to track technological innovations that facilitate the functioning of sick people, and the knowledge gained (e.g., regarding communication with attending staff) will be transferred to our volunteers and caregivers.

  • Task name: Representation of the "OSTATNIA WARTA" Foundation at nationwide long-term and senior care events in 2026.
  • Funding amount: 10,000.00 PLN
  • Total task value: 10,000.00 PLN
  • Date of contract signing: April 28, 2026

Accession to and active membership of the "OSTATNIA WARTA" Foundation in the European AGE Platform Europe network in 2026.

As an organization committed to the highest standards of care for the elderly and terminally ill, we strive to integrate into European structures. AGE Platform Europe is the largest European network of organizations of and for older people.

Our project includes formal accession to the organization and active participation in its key events in 2026. The transfer of EU know-how will allow us to even more effectively develop standards of care locally.

  • Task name: Accession to and active membership of the "OSTATNIA WARTA" Foundation in the European AGE Platform Europe network in 2026.
  • Funding amount: 19,530.00 PLN
  • Total task value: 19,530.00 PLN
  • Date of contract signing: May 7, 2026

Mobilna Warta – providing respite care and Sitting Service for seniors in the Pomeranian Voivodeship

As part of the "Mobilna Warta" (Mobile Watch) project, we are bringing the idea of a Sitting Service to homes throughout the Pomeranian Voivodeship. Our main tool is not medical intervention but compassionate presence and tailored support for seniors and terminally ill patients.

What do we do in practice?

  • For patients (the sick and dying): We break the barrier of isolation. We provide attentive companionship in the most difficult moments.
  • For families (actual caregivers): We offer professional respite care. We take the watch, giving loved ones time to regenerate and attend other responsibilities.
  • Implementation time: until December 31, 2026

We reach exactly where our presence is needed most.
